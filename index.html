About Us

Accelerating Africa's Participation in the Global Digital Economy

Bullion Bourse is a Zimbabwe-based technology and financial innovation company dedicated to accelerating Africa's participation in the global digital economy. We exist to close the technology divide between Africa and the First World by partnering with world-class innovative technology brands and deploying scalable, future-ready solutions across the continent.

In an era defined by digital transformation, we believe Africa must not follow innovation , it must lead it.

In Depth

What We Do

Bullion Bourse delivers high-impact digital and financial infrastructure solutions designed to empower businesses, institutions, entrepreneurs, and governments.

01

Digital Capital Formation

Through our international and local partners, we support the development and deployment of secure, transparent crowdfunding ecosystems that democratize access to capital across Africa.

We are proud to support Crowdaxe, Zimbabwe's first crowdfunding platform approved for sandbox testing by the Securities and Exchange Commission of Zimbabwe.

By aligning innovation with regulatory oversight, we help build trusted digital capital markets for Zimbabwe and beyond.

Our crowdfunding and capital formation solutions enable:

  • Startup and SME capital raising
  • Regulated sandbox innovation frameworks
  • Community and diaspora investment platforms
  • Social impact and infrastructure funding
  • Investor governance and compliance management
02

Capital Market Solutions

We facilitate innovative capital market infrastructure and digital financial instruments that enhance market accessibility and strengthen regulatory confidence.

Our focus is on building technology-enabled capital ecosystems that attract both domestic and international investors.

Our capital market solutions enhance:

  • Private capital raising platforms
  • Structured investment vehicles
  • Digital securities enablement
  • Alternative investment frameworks
  • Institutional-grade reporting and transparency systems
03

Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) Solutions

Under the banner of EthanetGroupe, through partnerships with leading global enterprise software providers, we deliver enterprise transformation solutions that enable organizations to operate at global standards.

Whether serving large enterprises with SAP S/4HANA or growing SMEs with SAP Business One, we empower organizations with integrated digital backbone systems that drive efficiency, governance, and scalability.

Our ERP capabilities include:

  • Financial management and reporting systems
  • Procurement and supply chain optimization
  • Production planning and manufacturing systems
  • Asset lifecycle management
  • Sales, distribution, and CRM integration
  • Real-time analytics and embedded intelligence
04

Payment Gateway Solutions

Under the banner of BullionBucks, we design and implement secure, scalable digital payment infrastructure that supports Africa's rapidly expanding digital commerce ecosystem and capital markets infrastructure.

Our payment solutions support:

  • Multi-currency transactions
  • Cross-border payment processing
  • E-commerce integration
  • Mobile and digital wallet enablement
  • Financial inclusion initiatives
